# Katanas and Shurikens

## Katana

This kind of weapon has high attack speed and deals a lot of damage when triggering its special ability.
To craft a katana, follow these steps:

1. Obtain [sand](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sand), [clay](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Clay), and [iron nuggets](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Iron_Nugget) to craft [iron sand](/wiki/materials/iron-sand) in the crafting table.
2. Once you have iron sand, put it inside a [furnace](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Furnace) so it melts into [tamahagane chunk](/wiki/materials/tamahagane-chunk).
3. Using two [tamahagane chunks](/wiki/materials/tamahagane-chunk) and a [stick](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Stick), craft a katana, just like this:

<img src="/assets/recipes/katana_recipe.png">

A katana is more durable than a regular [iron sword](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Iron_Sword). It can be repaired using tamahagane chunks or by enchanting it with [mending](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mending). All sword enchantments also work with a katana.

### Special Ability

Jump and hit an enemy while you're in the air to dash through them and deal even more damage. It works the same way as dealing critical damage with any weapon.

## Shuriken

This item can be thrown at enemies dealing damage and slowing them down for a few seconds. Up to 16 shurikens can be stacked at once, just like [snowballs](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Snowball).
To craft a shuriken, follow these steps:

1. Obtain [sand](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sand), [clay](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Clay), and [iron nuggets](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Iron_Nugget) to craft [iron sand](/wiki/materials/iron-sand) in the crafting table.
2. Once you have [iron sand](/wiki/materials/iron-sand), put it inside a [furnace](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Furnace) so it melts into [tamahagane chunk](/wiki/materials/tamahagane-chunk).
3. Using one [tamahagane chunk](/wiki/materials/tamahagane-chunk) will give 4 shurikens in total, just like this:

<img src="/assets/recipes/shuriken_recipe.png">

*Texture created by oRandomy.*

If a shuriken doesn't hit an enemy or any entity, it will not break and drop on the ground. [Boats](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Boat) and [item frames](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Item_Frame) cannot be broken using a shuriken. Enchantments for this item are not yet available.
