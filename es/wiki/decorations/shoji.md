# Shoji

### Screen

This screen or divider allows light sources to pass through and make any space look cozier. In real life, it is made of translucent rice paper and wood or bamboo, which makes the whole screen really lightweight.
To craft it, follow these steps:

1. Obtain [paper](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Paper) and [wood planks](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Planks) of any type.
2. Place the items on the [crafting table](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Crafting_Table) grid like this:
