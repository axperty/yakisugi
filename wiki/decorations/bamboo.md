# Bamboo

### Cladding Wall
This wall is made using [bamboo](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Block_of_Bamboo) or [stripped bamboo](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Block_of_Stripped_Bamboo). Its function is purely decorative and it allows the player to place them when trying to cover specific blocks.
To craft it, follow these steps:

1. Obtain [bamboo](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Block_of_Bamboo) or [stripped bamboo](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Block_of_Stripped_Bamboo).
