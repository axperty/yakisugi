# Aged Stone

This kind of stone is made by combining [cobblestone](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Cobblestone) and [stone](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Stone) together in the [crafting table](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Crafting_Table), it can be later be turned into polished aged stone using a [stonecutter](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Stonecutter). It allows the player to make decorations like [tsukubai](/wiki/decorations/tsukubai) and [stone lanterns](/wiki/decorations/stone-lantern). Its function is purely decorative.
To craft it, follow these steps:

1. Obtain [cobblestone](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Cobblestone) and [stone](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Stone).
