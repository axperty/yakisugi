# Tatami

This item is made using [string](https://minecraft.wiki/w/String) and [wheat straw](/wiki/materials/wheat-straw). It comes in two variants, tatami block and tatami mat. In real life, they are commonly used in Japanese houses or many different spaces. Its function is purely decorative.
