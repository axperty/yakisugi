# Stone Lantern

This lantern is made using [aged stone](/wiki/decorations/aged-stone) or polished aged stone. It's built from two parts, a base and a top. In real life, they originated in China, which were used for Buddhist temples and gardens. Some time later, and with the expansion of buddihsm, they were brought to Japan. Its function is purely decorative.
