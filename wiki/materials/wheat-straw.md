# Wheat Straw

[Wheat straw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Straw) is a material obtained by farming [wheat crops](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wheat) or breaking [grass](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Short_Grass), which can be later be used to make [straw armor](/wiki/weapons-and-armor/straw-armor) or [tatami](/wiki/decorations/tatami). It's fully compatible with [Farmer's Delight](https://github.com/vectorwing/FarmersDelight/wiki).
