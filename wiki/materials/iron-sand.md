# Iron Sand

[Iron sand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ironsand) is an item used to make [tamahagane chunk](/wiki/materials/tamahagane-chunk). It's made using [sand](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sand), [clay](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Clay), and [iron nuggets](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Iron_Nugget). In real life, it's been traditionally used in Japan to make *akame satetsu* and *masa satetsu*.
To craft it, follow these steps:

1. Obtain two [sand](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sand), two [clay](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Clay), and two [iron nuggets](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Iron_Nugget) to craft iron sand in the crafting table.
2. You will get iron sand.
