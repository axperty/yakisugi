# Tamahagane Chunk

[Tamahagane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamahagane) chunk is an item used to craft a [katana and shurikens](/wiki/weapons-and-armor/katanas-and-shurikens). It's made after melting [iron sand](/wiki/materials/iron-sand) inside a [furnace](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Furnace). In real life, it's been traditionally used to make a wide range of tools.
To craft it, follow these steps:

1. Put [iron sand](/wiki/materials/iron-sand) inside a furnace.
2. Wait a few seconds.
3. After it's fully melt, you will get tamahagane chunk.
