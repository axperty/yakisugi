# Charred and Slightly Charred Planks

This mod introduces the [yakisugi (Japanese: 焼 杉) method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yakisugi) for making these types of wood. You can't obtain charred and slightly charred wood from any trees or chests.
To craft them, follow these steps:

1. Craft and place a [campfire](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Campfire#Crafting) anywhere you want.
2. Use [flint and steel](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Flint_and_Steel#Crafting) to light it up.
3. Place [wooden planks](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Planks) on any adjacent side of the campfire, up to 3 blocks high.
4. Wait a few seconds for the [wooden planks](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Planks) to burn into slightly charred planks.
5. Grab an [axe](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Axe) and break the slightly charred planks block.

To get charred planks, skip step 5 and leave the slightly charred planks for a few more seconds so they burn even more. Once the planks are fully charred, make sure to break them quickly, because they will turn into [charcoal](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Charcoal) if you leave them burning for too long!

Charred and slightly charred planks are fire resistant and durable against explosions. Use it in the nether or near fire sources.

Charred and slightly charred planks can be crafted into things like slabs, stairs, trapdoors, fences, gates, buttons, signs, hanging signs, boats, and boats with chest:
