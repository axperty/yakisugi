# Straw Armor

Straw armor is made using [wheat straw](/wiki/materials/wheat-straw). It gives different effects when worn by the player. In real life, it's been used traditionally by Japanese farmers.
It consists of three pieces and it currently doesn't give any protection to the player, however, this will be added in a future update.

*Texture created by Lorenzo.*

### Straw Hat
This hat is crafted using wheat straw. When the player wears a straw hat, they will have a higher chance to get additional crops when breaking them. It works like the [looting](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Looting) enchantment.
To craft it, follow these steps:

1. Obtain [wheat straw](/wiki/materials/wheat-straw) by farming [wheat crops](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wheat) or breaking [grass](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Short_Grass).
