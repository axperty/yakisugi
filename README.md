### Overview

Yakisugi is a Japanese-inspired mod that adds charred wood, traditional decorations, straw armor, weapons, and many other features that
blend with the vanilla Minecraft style.

### Contributing

Your contributions allow Yakisugi to deliver a better gameplay experience for all players. Feel free to submit your code suggestions, bug reports, texture improvements, or translations.

### Features

- Slightly charred and charred wood planks crafted by placing wooden planks around a campfire
- Complete wood sets for slightly charred and charred variants.
- Katanas and shurikens crafted from iron sand and tamahagane chunks.
- Wheat straw obtained by breaking wheat crops or grass.
- Straw armor such as straw hat, straw mino, and straw boots.
- Shoji screens, shoji doors, and shoji bones.
- Bamboo and stripped bamboo cladding walls.
- Tatami and tatami mats crafted using wheat straw and string.
- Three types of paper lanterns with beautiful designs.
- Aged stone and polished aged stone sets crafted from cobblestone and stone.
- Stone lanterns which can hold lanterns inside when fully built.
