# Tsukubai

This decorative item is made using [aged stone](/wiki/decorations/aged-stone) or polished aged stone and [bamboo](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bamboo). In real life, it's provided at the entrance of a holy place so guests can wash their hands and rinse their mouths. Its function is purely decorative.
To craft it, follow these steps:

1. Obtain aged stone or polished aged stone and bamboo.
