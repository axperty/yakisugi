# Paper Lantern

This kind of lantern is made using a [candle](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Candle) and [paper](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Paper). There are three variants of them, the default one in white color, a cherry one in pink color with a cherry tree, and a mount Fuji one in light blue color with a mountain. In real life, they originated in China and it was then brought to Japan and nearby regions, where they gained their own aspect depending on the region where its crafted. Its function is purely decorative.
